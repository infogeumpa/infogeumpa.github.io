TikTok integration

How Info Geumpa uses the TikTok APIs, what it accesses, and what it does not.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Purpose

Info Geumpa uses a small publishing tool to post earthquake update videos to its own TikTok account, @infogeumpa. The tool uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. It exists only to publish Info Geumpa's own videos. It is not offered to the public and does not post on behalf of other people.

What the tool does

  1. Authorization. The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and grants the permissions listed below.
  2. Video creation. The tool prepares a short video from a BMKG, USGS or EMSC earthquake report: magnitude, time, location, depth and coordinates, with the source printed on screen.
  3. Review. The account owner reviews the video, caption and privacy setting. Nothing is posted automatically without this approval.
  4. Posting. After approval, the tool sends the video to TikTok through the Content Posting API. The post is labeled and credited to its data source in the caption.

Permissions (scopes) requested

ScopeWhy we need it
user.info.basicTo show the account owner which TikTok account is connected (display name and avatar) before posting.
video.publishTo publish approved earthquake update videos to @infogeumpa (Direct Post).
video.uploadTo send a video to the account owner's TikTok drafts when the owner prefers to finish publishing inside TikTok.

What we do not do

  • We do not read, download or store other people's videos, comments, messages or follower lists.
  • We do not post without the account owner's approval.
  • We do not sell, share or use TikTok data for advertising, profiling or training of AI models.
  • We do not use the API for engagement manipulation, spam or automated follows and likes.

Data handling

The tool stores only what it needs to keep the connection working: the account owner's TikTok open ID and the OAuth access and refresh tokens, kept in a private, access-restricted store. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Disconnecting

The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services. On revocation, the stored tokens are deleted. You can also email infogeumpa@gmail.com and we will delete them.
