About Info Geumpa

An independent channel that turns official earthquake reports into short, readable videos.

What we do

Info Geumpa (geumpa means "earthquake" in Acehnese) publishes short videos about recent earthquakes in the Indonesian region. Each video shows the magnitude, time, location, depth and coordinates, and, when the source reports it, where the shaking was felt. The map zooms to the epicenter so viewers can see where it happened.

Where the data comes from

All figures come from three agencies: BMKG (Indonesia, our primary source), the USGS (United States) and the EMSC (Europe). The source is printed on every video. We do not measure earthquakes ourselves and we do not publish predictions.

Our commitments

  • Source first. Every figure is compared with the agency's report before posting. For Indonesia, BMKG is the reference.
  • No rounding for effect. Magnitude and depth are shown as the agency reports them.
  • No fear-mongering. No predictions, rumors or unconfirmed claims.
  • Corrections. If the source revises a figure, we update the video and say so.
  • Human approval. Videos are posted to @infogeumpa only after the account owner approves them.
We are not an early-warning service.Info Geumpa is independent and is not affiliated with BMKG, the USGS or the EMSC. In an emergency, follow instructions from BMKG and your local disaster management agency (BPBD).
