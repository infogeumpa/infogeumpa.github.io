Privacy Policy

What this site and our publishing tool collect, and what they do not.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who we are

Info Geumpa is an independent earthquake information channel (TikTok: @infogeumpa). Contact: infogeumpa@gmail.com.

This website

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies and we do not track visitors. We do not ask you to create an account or submit personal data on this site.

The example video on the home page loads on your device and requests recent earthquake data from BMKG, USGS and EMSC, and typefaces from Google Fonts. Those services receive your IP address and browser details as part of the request, under their own privacy policies. The page also works with the saved example if those requests fail. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Our TikTok publishing tool

The tool is used only by the Info Geumpa account owner. When the owner connects TikTok, we receive and store:

  • the TikTok open ID, display name and avatar of the connected account (user.info.basic);
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, so the tool can post approved videos.

We use this data only to publish Info Geumpa's earthquake videos to the connected account. We do not sell or share it, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling or AI training. We do not collect data about TikTok viewers, followers or other users.

Earthquake data

Earthquake reports come from BMKG, USGS and EMSC. They describe natural events and contain no personal data.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the account is connected and deleted when access is revoked. To revoke access, use TikTok's Apps and services settings, or email us. We will delete stored tokens and account identifiers within 30 days of your request.

Security

Stored tokens are kept in a private, access-restricted store and are never published in this repository or on this site.

Children

This site and tool are not directed to children and do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing infogeumpa@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
